Collagora Support

Help and frequently asked questions for Collagora.

Collagora helps you turn your photos into collages, posters, and edited photos with layouts, text, stickers, and filters on Android. Your projects stay on your device.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where are my projects stored?

Projects, and the photos you add to them, are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. Collagora has no account and does not upload your projects or photos.

Why does Collagora ask to read my photos?

Collagora has a built-in photo browser so you can pick several photos at once for a collage. Photos are read only on your device and are never uploaded. On Android 14 and later, you can choose to allow only selected photos, and Collagora will see only those.

I can't see all my photos. How do I change photo access?

If you allowed only selected photos, or declined access, open Settings in Collagora and tap Read Photos, or go to your device's Settings app > Apps > Collagora > Permissions > Photos and videos, and choose the access you want.

Where do saved images go?

Saved images are added to your device's photo gallery, where you can find them in your Gallery or Photos app. On Android 9 and earlier, Collagora needs storage permission to save. If saving fails, check Settings in Collagora > Save to Album. You can also use Share to send an image directly to another app.

Do exported images include location or camera details?

No. Exported images are newly rendered and do not include EXIF metadata such as location from the original photos.

How do I delete my projects?

On the home screen, touch and hold a recent project to delete it. This also deletes the photo copies stored in it. To delete every project at once, open Settings in Collagora and tap Clear All Projects. Uninstalling Collagora deletes all of its data. Images you saved to your gallery and the original photos are not affected.

How do I free up storage?

Open Settings in Collagora to see how much space the app uses. Clear Cache removes temporary files without affecting your projects. Clear All Projects deletes every project and the photo copies stored in them.

Do my projects sync across devices?

No. Projects stay on the device where you created them. If Android backup is turned on, Android may include Collagora data in your device backup according to your device settings.

Why do some fonts need an internet connection?

To keep the app small, some fonts are downloaded from Google Fonts the first time they are needed and then saved on your device. Make sure you are connected to the internet. If a font does not appear, try again later.

Can I import my own filters?

Yes. Collagora can import color filters in the .cube (3D LUT) format. Use the import option in the filter panel and choose a .cube file on your device. Imported filters stay on your device.

I found a bug or have a feature request.

We'd love to hear from you. Email us and include your Collagora version (shown in Settings), your Android version, and your device model, with a short description of the issue. Screenshots help a lot.

常见问题

我的作品保存在哪里？

作品以及加入作品的照片只保存在设备上的应用私有存储中。Collagora 没有账号， 不会上传你的作品或照片。

为什么需要读取照片的权限？

Collagora 内置了照片浏览器，方便你一次为拼贴选择多张照片。照片只在你的设备上读取， 不会被上传。在 Android 14 及以上版本，你可以只允许访问选中的照片，Collagora 只能看到这些照片。

看不到全部照片，如何更改照片权限？

如果你只允许了部分照片或拒绝了访问，可以在 Collagora 的“设置”中点按“读取照片”， 或前往系统“设置”>“应用”>“Collagora”>“权限”>“照片和视频”，选择需要的访问方式。

保存的图片在哪里？

保存的图片会添加到设备相册，可以在“相册”或“图库”应用中找到。在 Android 9 及以下版本， 保存需要存储权限；如果保存失败，请在 Collagora 的“设置”中检查“保存到相册”。 你也可以通过“分享”把图片直接发送到其他应用。

导出的图片会包含地点或相机信息吗？

不会。导出的图片由应用重新绘制生成，不包含原照片中的地点等 EXIF 元数据。

如何删除我的作品？

在首页长按最近的作品即可删除，作品中保存的照片副本会一并删除。如需一次删除全部作品， 请在 Collagora 的“设置”中点按“清除全部工程”。卸载 Collagora 会删除它的全部数据。 已保存到相册的图片和原图不受影响。

如何释放存储空间？

在 Collagora 的“设置”中可以查看应用占用的空间。“清除缓存”会删除临时文件，不影响你的作品； “清除全部工程”会删除所有作品及其中保存的照片副本。

作品会在多台设备间同步吗？

不会。作品只保存在创建它的设备上。如果开启了 Android 备份，Android 可能会按你的设备设置 把 Collagora 的数据包含在设备备份中。

为什么有些字体需要联网？

为了减小应用体积，部分字体会在首次需要时从 Google Fonts 下载并保存在设备上。 请确认设备已连接网络；如果字体没有显示，请稍后重试。

可以导入自己的滤镜吗？

可以。Collagora 支持导入 .cube（3D LUT）格式的颜色滤镜。在滤镜面板中使用导入功能， 选择设备上的 .cube 文件即可。导入的滤镜只保存在你的设备上。
