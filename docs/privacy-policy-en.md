# Collagora Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

## Overview

Collagora is an Android app for making photo collages, posters, and edited photos. This policy explains what data Collagora processes, why, where it is stored, and the choices you have.

Your photos and projects are processed and stored on your device. Collagora has no account system, does not upload your photos or projects to our servers, and we do not sell your personal information.

## Data the App Processes

### Photos in Your Library

Collagora has a built-in photo browser so you can pick several photos at once for a collage. To show it, the app asks for permission to read the images on your device:

- On Android 14 and later, you can allow access to all photos or only to the photos you select. If you choose selected photos, the app can see only those.
- On Android 13, the app asks for the "Photos" (read images) permission.
- On Android 12 and earlier, the app asks for storage read access, which Android uses for photos.

Some features, such as choosing a single background image, use the Android system photo picker instead. In that case the app receives only the photo you pick.

The app reads photos only to show thumbnails, let you choose them, and edit them. Photos are never uploaded.

### Your Projects

When you add photos to a project, Collagora copies them into the app's private storage on your device, so your project keeps working even if the original is later edited or deleted. Layouts, text, stickers, filters, backgrounds, and project thumbnails are stored there as well. Other apps cannot access this storage.

### Imported Filter Files

If you import a color filter (a `.cube` file), Collagora opens the Android system file picker. The app reads only the file you choose and keeps the filter on your device.

### App Preferences

Your settings, recently used colors, and recently used stickers are stored on your device to make the app easier to use. They are not sent anywhere.

### Save to Gallery

When you save a finished image, Collagora adds it to your device's photo gallery. On Android 10 and later this does not need an extra permission. On Android 9 and earlier, Android asks for storage write permission.

Exported images are newly rendered by the app and do not include EXIF metadata such as location or camera details from the original photos.

### Sharing

When you tap Share, Collagora opens the Android share sheet. The exported image goes only to the destination you choose, and that destination handles it under its own privacy policy.

### Font Downloads

To keep the app small, some fonts, including fonts used in the app's interface and some fonts in the text tool, are downloaded from Google Fonts the first time they are needed and then cached on your device. When a font is downloaded, your device connects to Google's servers, which receive standard connection information such as your IP address. Google handles these requests under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). The requests do not include your photos or projects.

### Links

When you open a link from the app, such as this policy or an email address, Collagora hands it to your browser or email app, which handles it under its own privacy policy.

## Third-Party Services

The current version of Collagora does not include analytics, advertising, or crash reporting services.

Future versions may add services such as crash diagnostics, usage analytics, or advertising. Before such a version is released, we will update this policy to describe each service, the data it receives, why, and how you can control it, and we will update the app's Data safety information on Google Play. Where the law or Google Play's rules require your consent, the app will ask for it first.

These services will not receive your projects or the photos you add to them.

## Data Storage, Retention, and Deletion

Your projects and the photos in them are stored only on your device:

- Deleting a project in the app removes its project files, including the photo copies stored in that project.
- In the app's Settings, you can clear the cache or delete all local projects.
- Uninstalling Collagora removes all of its data from your device.
- Images you saved to your gallery and the original photos on your device are managed by Android and are not affected by deleting a project or uninstalling the app.
- If Android backup is turned on, Android may include Collagora data in your device backup according to your device settings. Google handles those backups.

## Permissions

- **Photos / storage read:** lets you browse and select photos for your projects. You can choose to allow only selected photos on Android 14 and later.
- **Storage write (Android 9 and earlier only):** saves finished images to your gallery.
- **Internet:** downloads fonts as described above.

You can change or revoke these permissions at any time in your device's Settings app. Collagora does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, or contacts.

## Children's Privacy

Collagora is a general-audience app and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
