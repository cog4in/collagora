# Collagora Public Privacy Policy

This repository contains the public privacy policy pages for Collagora. It contains no app source code.

Public URLs (GitHub Pages):

- Main page: `https://cog4in.github.io/collagora/`
- English privacy policy: `https://cog4in.github.io/collagora/docs/privacy-policy-en`
- Chinese privacy policy: `https://cog4in.github.io/collagora/docs/privacy-policy-zh`

Publication requirements:

- Serve these files from a public HTTPS URL without login, payment, or region restrictions. Google Play requires the privacy policy URL to be publicly accessible and not a PDF.
- Keep the English and Chinese policies in sync, and update the "Last updated" date on every material change.
- Keep the policy aligned with the app's actual data handling, its Android permissions (`AndroidManifest.xml`), and the Google Play Data safety form.
- Before releasing a version that adds a third-party SDK (for example Firebase or an ad network), add a section for each service to both policies and update the Data safety form.
- The app's in-app privacy policy (`assets/legal/privacy_policy.*.md`) should match these pages.
